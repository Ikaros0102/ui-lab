Titus Lucretius Carus (Ferrandus Press, Brescia 1473)
N othing can be created out of nothing, nor can things once begotten be resolved back into naught. Minute primordial bodies drift through the boundless celestial void in perpetual motion, colliding and swerving by an infinitesimal clinamen to weave the fabric of worlds.
