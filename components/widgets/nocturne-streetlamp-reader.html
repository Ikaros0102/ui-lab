Rainer Maria Rilke • Book of Hours

The evening is folding into violet shadows around the dripping iron eaves. Streetlamps flicker into warm sodium brilliance along the wet cobblestone boulevard, scattering golden amber flares into the mist.

"Quiet friend of many distances, feel how your breath

Underneath the shelter of the gaslamp, the city recedes into a soft distant murmur of carriage wheels and rain on black asphalt. Reading in this amber halo, the hours slow to a tranquil stillness.
