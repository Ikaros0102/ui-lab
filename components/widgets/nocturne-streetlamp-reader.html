Evening Promenade in Fog

Rainer Maria Rilke • Book of Hours

The evening is folding into violet shadows around the dripping iron eaves. Three gas mantles hiss softly inside their beveled glass cages, pouring cones of warm amber brilliance down onto the wet cobblestone boulevard.

“Quiet friend of many distances, feel how your breath
enlarges all of space. Let this darkness be a bell tower
and you the bell.”

Underneath the shelter of the candelabrum, each falling raindrop catches the golden beam for a fleeting millisecond before vanishing into the dark puddles. In this amber halo, the hours slow to a tranquil stillness.
